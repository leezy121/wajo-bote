## How the program works
- Goal: We're aiming for 30 videos a month across the program. We'll agree on your individual posting schedule in Discord.
- Your pages: Create a new, separate Wajo-focused page on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
- Your role: Film, edit and post each video on your pages. (In this server: send every video to Leez for review first.)

- Product access: Ask in the Discord if you need access to Wajo or help using Fo.
- After posting: Share the live links in Discord.
- CTA: We'll tell you which CTA to use for each video. This could be "Check out the link in my bio" or a comment prompt connected to a lead magnet.

## What is Wajo?
Wajo gives you an AI agent called Fo. You tell Fo something you need to get done, and Fo helps you do it.
For example, you can ask Fo to:
- Find a haircut appointment that fits your schedule.
- Find a restaurant for you and your friends.
- Contact a business about availability.
- Help you deal with a bill or charge.
- Chase a refund.
- Follow up on an unanswered email.
- Reschedule an appointment.
- Keep track of appointments and deadlines.
- Help organise a booking.

## What should every video show?
Keep it simple.
1. Show the problem. Tell us what you need to get done. Examples: "I've needed to book a haircut for two weeks." / "My internet bill just went up." / "I told my friends I'd book dinner and completely forgot."
2. Ask Fo to help. Open Wajo and give Fo the task. Talk to Fo normally. You don't need to write a perfect prompt.
3. Show Fo working. Screen record what happens. If Fo gives you options, show them. If Fo asks you a question, answer it. If Fo needs you to approve something, show yourself approving it.
4. Show the result. Show what actually happened: an appointment option, a booking, a response from a business, a restaurant you found, a rescheduled appointment, a refund update, a clear next step.
5. Show your reaction. Tell us what you think. Keep it natural.

## How to film
Every video should include: You. The problem. You using Fo. Fo's response. The result. Your reaction.
You can film yourself talking to camera, film your phone or laptop and use screen recordings. You can also put screenshots or screen recordings of Fo beside you while you talk.
Use the real result. Only say Fo booked, called, sent, paid or completed something if it actually did.

## Choose a video concept
We're recreating eight proven Instagram Reel formats, adapted to show how people can use Fo from Wajo to explore ways of making or saving money.
All videos are aimed at American audiences. Use US locations, dollars and American businesses.
Your job is to recreate your chosen reference as closely as possible, not create a completely new video.

## IMPORTANT: Recreate everything you see in the reference
Before filming, watch your assigned reference at least three times.
We want a 1:1 recreation of the reference's structure, visuals, delivery and editing, adapted to Wajo.

Visual hooks (the first three seconds are especially important):
- Copy the original opening shot and camera angle.
- Match what the creator is wearing or holding where practical.
- Match their body positioning, gestures, facial expressions and energy.
- Recreate the opening on-screen text, including its placement, size, colors and animation.
- Match the timing of the first cut or transition.
- If the original starts with a reaction, laptop reveal or screen recording, yours should too.
- Don't replace the original visual hook with a generic talking-head introduction.

Throughout the video:
- On-screen text: Recreate the captions, headlines, numbers, labels and text animations. Adapt the wording where necessary for Wajo.
- Screen recordings: Match the sequence of websites, screenshots, scrolling, clicking and demonstrations.
- Editing: Match the pacing, jump cuts, zooms, transitions, sound effects and visual changes.
- Delivery: Match the speaking speed, tone, gestures and facial expressions.
- Visual examples: If the original shows sales, views, products, websites or calculations, recreate that type of visual using accurate information.
- Fo demonstration: Show the actual Fo interface, the full prompt being entered and the response it produces.

Scripts:
- Read your assigned script word for word.
- Anything in [square brackets] is a filming instruction. Do not read it aloud.
- When a Fo prompt appears, copy the entire prompt exactly.
- Do not skip lines, remove demonstrations or replace specific instructions with generic footage.

Before submitting, play your video beside the original reference and check:
- Does the first three seconds look and feel like the original?
- Have you recreated every important on-screen visual?
- Does your script follow the same sequence?
- Have you included all the Fo demonstrations?
- Does your editing match the reference's pacing?

The script tells you what to say. The reference tells you how the video should look.
Use your own footage and appropriately licensed assets. Do not present another creator's earnings as your own, fabricate results or promise guaranteed income.
